## Borrowing (https://www.uottawa.ca/library/services/borrow)

- A valid library card is required. Undergraduates: 120 days, 500 items, unlimited renewals.
- Periodicals, law reports and games: normally 14 days with 2 renewals.
- Most media (DVD, CD) and equipment: 14 days with 2 renewals.
- **Multimedia equipment from the Morisset Library main service desk: 7 days with 2 renewals, limited to current students, faculty and employees.**
- Place a hold, interlibrary loan (18 Omni partners, nearly 25.3 million resources), and home delivery (in Canada, outside Ottawa/Gatineau) exist.

## Computers (https://www.uottawa.ca/library/services/computers-technology)

- Public computers at Morisset, Health Sciences and Brian Dickson libraries, first come first served, sign in with uoAccess.
- uOLabsPlus: licensed software on your own device (ArcGIS, Excel, SPSS, Stata, Word and others), shut down midnight to 3 a.m. daily. Stata has 2 simultaneous licences.
- MRT 308: geographic and statistical software. Morisset 3rd floor: 4 PCs with Adobe (free Adobe account needed).

## Study rooms (https://www.uottawa.ca/library/services/reserve-study-space)

Bookable online at Morisset, Brian Dickson Law, Learning Crossroads (5th floor) and Health Sciences. Rooms hold up to about 6 people; sizes vary (Library home page). Carrels are for graduate students.

## Print and scan (https://www.uottawa.ca/library/services/print-copy-scan)

- Black and white: $0.09 one-sided, $0.16 two-sided. Colour: $0.25 one-sided, $0.48 two-sided. Scanning is free.
- Add funds to your student card online, at the uOttawa Card Service offices, or at a Card Loader.
- Self-serve book scanner: Morisset 1st floor photocopier room.

## Writing and citing (https://www.uottawa.ca/library/writing-citing)

Online tutorials and hands-on workshops, including citation styles, citation software and avoiding plagiarism. Also the Academic Writing Help Centre and Ask a librarian.

## Other services (https://www.uottawa.ca/library/services)

Digital Skills Hub (podcasting, VR, other technology), course reserves, scan-on-demand, copyright, accessibility services, research guides and librarians (https://www.uottawa.ca/library/research-guides-librarians), workshops (https://www.uottawa.ca/library/workshops-orientations-tours).
