# First Chapter: demo now

## P0
Help a first-year student find a Library service quickly, understand the next step, and reach the official service. The native iOS app is the demo; the separate Downloads/first-chapter folder contains an earlier web prototype and source research.

## 60-second demo
1. Open First Chapter in the iPhone 17 Pro Simulator. Skip onboarding if time is short.
2. Find: search **camera**. Open **Borrow a Camera or Gear**. Show the answer, loan details and official equipment action.
3. Tap **Add to My Plan**. Open **My Plan**: the saved service is there. Mark complete, then undo to show it is editable.
4. Borrow: show seven equipment categories. Each opens the official equipment catalogue, which owns availability and borrowing.
5. Ask: try **How do I cite my sources?**. Read the source label: fallback is Library search, not AI.

## 30-second pitch
“First-year students often do not know that the Library offers cameras, recording studios, study rooms and citation help. First Chapter starts with their needs and puts the answer on the service list. Students can open the official next step, save a personal plan, and explore services they did not know existed. The plan stays on the device. We do not pretend to reserve rooms or know live equipment availability.”

## Honest boundaries
- Catalogue and local plan work without an AI key.
- DeepSeek is optional and is not required for the demo. This Downloads snapshot contains no Secrets.plist or API key.
- Reservations, sign-in and live availability belong to official university systems.
- Any website click-saving number is an estimate, not a measured usability outcome.
- Simulator build/run and source review do not prove real-device encryption or production readiness.
